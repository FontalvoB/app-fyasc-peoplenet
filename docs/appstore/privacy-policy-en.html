The data controller is your employing organization; Fya Social Capital acts as the platform provider. Processing is based on the performance of the employment relationship and the legitimate interest of people management, under the data protection laws applicable to your country.

All communication uses encrypted HTTPS. Platform access is protected by authentication and role control. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode and never sharing your credentials.
